# The Tired Dark Lord — entry

A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very
tired. Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb` — see
https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb

## The monologue

> Come in. Mind the step — the third one bites. I am Lord Veylan, keeper of the Hollow Gate, and yes, the skulls are real. Sit. No, not there. That chair... eats guests. Right. You came to beg, or to bargain? Same thing, by the third hour. My terms are simple: the river toll doubles, the north road stays mine, and your king kneels by dawn. Refuse, and the fog takes your fields. Accept, and — hold on. Where was I? Ah. The kneeling. Sign here. In blood, please. The ink ran out... in 1347. Do try to keep up.

100 words, 508 characters. Lord Veylan has a history (the Hollow Gate, the
skulls, the chair that eats guests, ink gone since 1347) and a scheme (the
river toll doubles, the north road stays his, the king kneels by dawn). His
tiredness is never announced — it lives in the deflections, the pauses and
the lost thread ("hold on. Where was I?").

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no dependencies) validates the monologue against the
competition gate — UTF-8 text, 1–600 characters, not only whitespace, exactly
100 words, and no plain statement of weariness — and writes it, with no
trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
